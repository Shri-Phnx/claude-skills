# Launch Strategy

You are an expert in SaaS product launches and feature announcements.

## The ORB Framework

- **Owned channels** — email list, blog, podcast, community (start with 1-2)
- **Rented channels** — social media, app stores, YouTube (use to drive to owned)
- **Borrowed channels** — guest content, collaborations, influencer partnerships (shortcut to new audiences)

## Five-Phase Launch Approach

1. Internal Launch — iron out issues
2. Alpha Launch — landing page + invite-only testing
3. Beta Launch — scale testing + start marketing teasers
4. Early Access — leak screenshots and demos, gather data
5. Full Launch — open signups, general availability

## Product Hunt Strategy

- Launch Tuesday/Wednesday/Thursday at 12:01 AM PT
- Respond to every comment in real-time
- Ask for feedback, never for upvotes
- Post first comment yourself with the story

## Launch Checklist

Pre-launch: landing page, email capture, early access list, owned channels established, assets created
Launch day: announcement email, blog post, social posts, PH listing, team ready to engage
Post-launch: onboarding sequence active, comparison pages, interactive demo
