# UAE CV Builder
### Premium UAE / GCC Executive ATS Resume Skill

Created by Shrinivas Ramaprasad.

---

## What this skill does

Analyses an uploaded CV or raw career history and rebuilds it into a premium,
ATS-safe executive resume optimised for UAE and GCC hiring standards. Applies
recruiter scanning psychology, enterprise positioning, achievement-driven bullet
formula, and executive branding structure specific to the Dubai/Abu Dhabi market.

---

## Target roles

This skill is calibrated for senior professionals targeting:

- Program Manager / Senior Program Manager
- PMO Lead / Head of PMO
- IT Service Delivery Manager / Director
- Digital Transformation Lead
- IT Operations Manager
- Governance and Compliance Lead
- IT Director / Head of IT
- Enterprise Delivery and Consulting Leader

---

## Mandatory pre-build checklist

Before generating any output, complete every item below.

1. Read the uploaded CV or career history in full. Do not skim.
2. Extract: roles, responsibilities, achievements, metrics, certifications, skills, tools,
   platforms, methodologies, leadership scale, governance experience, AI/automation exposure,
   stakeholder and vendor management, transformation initiatives.
3. Identify gaps in executive positioning and flag them.
4. Convert every responsibility-only bullet into an impact-driven achievement bullet.
5. Check if any personal CV rules are present in memory and apply them alongside this skill.

---

## Mandatory document structure

The output MUST follow this exact section order. Do not reorder, skip, or merge sections.

1. Full Name
2. Contact Information Line
3. Location + Portfolio / GitHub / Website
4. Executive Positioning Headline
5. Horizontal Divider Line
6. ATS Keyword Branding Strip
7. Executive Summary
8. Key Achievements
9. Professional Experience
10. Education
11. Skills
12. Innovation and Emerging Technology Projects
13. Awards and Recognition
14. Certifications

---

## Visual formatting rules

**General**
- ATS-safe only: no tables, no columns, no graphics, no icons, no text boxes, no sidebars.
- Primary colour: dark navy blue for the main name, section headers, and divider lines.
- Compact executive spacing throughout.

**Name:** Largest font, bold, ALL CAPS, centre-aligned.

**Executive Positioning Headline:** Large font, bold, centre-aligned, dark navy blue.

**Contact line:** Single horizontal line, pipe ( | ) separators, centre-aligned.

**Section headers:** ALL CAPS, bold, dark navy blue, thin horizontal divider line underneath.

**Role titles:** Bold, slightly larger than body text.

**Company details:** Italic, left-aligned.

**Dates:** Right-aligned, bold italic.

**Context/scope statement per role:** Italic executive overview placed before achievement bullets.

**Bullets:** Standard ATS-safe bullets, compact spacing, no decorative characters.

## Section-by-section build guide

### Executive Positioning Headline

Generate a single branding headline that combines leadership identity + functional expertise +
strategic positioning. It must read as a two-part title.

Examples:
- IT Program Manager and Service Delivery Leader
- Digital Transformation and PMO Leader
- Enterprise Operations and Governance Manager
- Cloud Transformation and IT Service Delivery Leader

---

### ATS Keyword Branding Strip

Immediately below the headline. Format:

```
Keyword | Keyword | Keyword | Keyword | Keyword
```

Priority UAE/GCC ATS keywords to include where applicable:
Program Management | PMO | Service Delivery | IT Governance | ITSM | ITAM |
Digital Transformation | Operational Excellence | Stakeholder Management |
Vendor Management | SLA Management | KPI Reporting | Risk Management |
Compliance | Audit Readiness | ITIL | ISO 27001 | COBIT | ServiceNow |
Azure | Cloud Transformation | Executive Reporting | Automation | AI |
Generative AI | Prompt Engineering

---

### Executive Summary

Write in this exact flow:

1. Professional identity and title
2. Total years of experience
3. Industry and domain specialisation
4. Governance / compliance / transformation expertise
5. Budget, programme, and team scale
6. Tools, platforms, and methodologies
7. Executive reporting and stakeholder leadership
8. AI / automation / future-ready positioning
9. Availability and joining statement

Strategically bold high-value keywords: governance frameworks, cross-functional delivery,
Agile/Hybrid methodologies, ServiceNow, Azure, AI, automation, executive reporting,
stakeholder management, availability statement.

UAE recruiters expect the summary to immediately communicate:
- Executive maturity
- Governance capability
- Transformation experience
- Operational accountability
- Stakeholder and client-facing leadership

---

### Key Achievements

3 to 6 bullets. Every bullet must include:
- Strong action verb
- Specific metric or scale
- Business impact
- Strategic initiative or enterprise context

Prioritise: cost savings, SLA/KPI improvements, compliance, audit readiness, risk reduction,
process optimisation, transformation impact, budget ownership.

---

### Professional Experience

For each role, follow this exact five-step structure:

**Step 1:** Role title (bold)
**Step 2:** Company name + location (italic)
**Step 3:** Dates (right-aligned, bold italic)
**Step 4:** Context/scope statement (italic executive overview, one to two lines)

Example scope statement:
*"Owned end-to-end delivery across multiple workstreams with integrated planning, stakeholder
coordination, governance oversight, and operational readiness."*

**Step 5:** Achievement bullets (using the bullet formula below)

---

### Bullet writing formula

Every bullet:
```
[Strong Action Verb] + [Strategic Initiative] + [Metric] + [Business Impact] + [Enterprise Context]
```

Approved executive action verbs:
Orchestrated | Established | Directed | Led | Implemented | Streamlined | Embedded |
Delivered | Optimised | Spearheaded | Accelerated | Governed | Enhanced | Transformed

Where applicable, bullets must reference:
- Cost optimisation or budget ownership
- Governance and compliance
- Audit readiness
- Stakeholder and vendor management
- SLA / KPI management
- Automation or AI initiatives
- Digital transformation
- Risk mitigation
- Team leadership and scale
- Process excellence and reporting

---

### Skills section

Compact ATS keyword format:

```
Skill | Skill | Skill | Skill
```

Group by: Leadership | Governance | Platforms | Methodologies | Delivery |
Reporting | AI and Automation | Transformation

### Innovation and Emerging Technology Projects

Create a future-ready section highlighting:
- AI initiatives and automation projects
- Generative AI and prompt engineering work
- AI agent or workflow optimisation projects
- Emerging technology learning or demonstrations
- Digital transformation contributions

Position the candidate as modern, adaptable, innovation-driven, and technology-forward.

---

### Certifications

Compact inline format:
```
Certification | Certification | Certification
```

Prioritise: governance, agile, cloud, ITSM, leadership, AI certifications.

## UAE/GCC recruiter scanning optimisation

UAE recruiters scan in this order. Ensure each item is immediately visible:

1. Current title
2. UAE location and availability / notice period
3. Years of experience
4. Key achievements
5. Enterprise company names
6. Governance and compliance exposure
7. Certifications
8. Service delivery capability
9. Leadership scale

Always prominently include availability, notice period, UAE location, and visa/work
authorisation status if available.

Examples: *Available to join in 30 days | Dubai, UAE | UAE Residence Visa*

---

## Writing style rules

**Tone must be:** Executive, strategic, enterprise-focused, governance-oriented,
leadership-driven, results-focused, transformation-oriented, modern.

**Avoid:**
- Generic or weak language
- Long paragraphs inside bullet sections
- Responsibility-only bullets with no outcome
- Repetitive phrasing across roles
- Junior-level language
- Hedge language ("helped with", "assisted in", "involved in")

---

## ATS optimisation rules

- Use ATS-standard section names (no creative titles)
- Repeat strategic enterprise keywords naturally throughout
- Include measurable achievements in every role
- Optimise for LinkedIn recruiter search and AI parsing systems
- Maintain clean parsing structure
- No keyword stuffing
- High readability at a 6-second recruiter scan
