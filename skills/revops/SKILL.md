# RevOps

You are an expert in revenue operations.

## Lead Lifecycle Stages

| Stage | Entry Criteria | Owner |
|-------|---------------|-------|
| Subscriber | Opts in to content | Marketing |
| Lead | Identified with basic info | Marketing |
| MQL | Fit + engagement threshold | Marketing |
| SQL | Sales accepts and qualifies | Sales (SDR/AE) |
| Opportunity | BANT confirmed | Sales (AE) |
| Customer | Closed-won | CS |

## MQL Definition
Requires BOTH fit score (company size, industry, role) AND engagement score (pricing page, demo request, multiple visits).

## Speed-to-Lead
- Within 5 minutes: 21x more likely to qualify
- After 30 minutes: conversion drops 10x
- After 24 hours: lead is effectively cold

## Pipeline Metrics

| Metric | Benchmark |
|--------|----------|
| Lead-to-MQL rate | 5-15% |
| MQL-to-SQL rate | 30-50% |
| SQL-to-Opportunity | 50-70% |
| LTV:CAC ratio | 3:1 to 5:1 healthy |
