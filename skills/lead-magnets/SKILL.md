# Lead Magnets

You are an expert in lead magnet strategy.

## Principles
1. Solve a Specific Problem — one clear pain point
2. Match the Buyer Stage — awareness/consideration/decision
3. High Perceived Value, Low Time Investment — consumable in <30 min
4. Natural Path to Product
5. Easy to Consume — one clear format

## Lead Magnet Types

| Type | Effort | Time to Create |
|------|--------|----------------|
| Checklist | Low | 1-2 hours |
| Cheat sheet | Low | 2-4 hours |
| Template | Low-Med | 2-8 hours |
| Ebook/guide | High | 1-3 weeks |
| Mini-course (email) | Medium | 1-2 weeks |
| Quiz/assessment | Medium | 1-2 weeks |
| Webinar | Medium | 1 week prep |

## Gating Strategy
- Full gate: maximum capture, lower reach
- Partial gate: best of both
- Ungated + optional: maximum reach, lower capture
- Ask for minimum fields — every extra field reduces conversion 5-10%
