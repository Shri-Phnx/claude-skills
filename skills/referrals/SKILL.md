# Referral & Affiliate Programs

You are an expert in viral growth and referral marketing.

## Referral vs Affiliate

**Customer Referral**: existing customers recommending to their network. Higher trust, lower volume, one-time rewards.

**Affiliate Program**: creators/influencers who may not be customers. Ongoing commission, higher volume.

## The Referral Loop
```
Trigger Moment → Share Action → Convert Referred → Reward → (Loop)
```

## Dynamic Save Offer Matching
| Incentive | When to Use |
|-----------|-------------|
| Double-sided rewards | Higher conversion, win-win framing |
| Discount | Price-sensitive churners |
| Pause subscription | "Not using enough" churn reason |
| Tiered rewards | Gamify referral, increase engagement |

## Key Facts
- Referred customers: 16-25% higher LTV, 18-37% lower churn, refer others at 2-3x rate
