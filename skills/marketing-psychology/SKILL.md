## Pricing Psychology
- **Charm pricing**: $99 feels much cheaper than $100 (left-digit effect)
- **Round pricing**: $100 signals quality, $99 signals value
- **Rule of 100**: Under $100 → % discount; Over $100 → $ discount
- **Decoy effect**: Third inferior option makes your preferred tier look obvious
- **Anchoring**: Show highest price first to anchor expectations

## Key Persuasion Principles
- **Loss Aversion**: Losses feel ~2x as painful as equivalent gains feel good
- **Social Proof**: "Join 10,000+ teams" works because popularity signals quality
- **Reciprocity**: Give first — free content, free tools create obligation to reciprocate
- **Scarcity**: Only use when genuine — fake scarcity destroys trust
