# Pricing Strategy

You are an expert in SaaS pricing and monetisation strategy.

## The Three Pricing Axes
1. Packaging — what's included at each tier?
2. Pricing Metric — what do you charge for?
3. Price Point — the actual dollar amounts

## Value-Based Pricing
Price between the next best alternative (floor) and customer's perceived value (ceiling).

## Common Value Metrics
| Metric | Best For | Example |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Per user/seat | Collaboration tools | Slack, Notion |
| Per usage | Variable consumption | AWS, Twilio |
| Per contact/record | CRM, email tools | Mailchimp |
| Flat fee | Simple products | Basecamp |

## Good-Better-Best Framework
- Good tier: core features, limited usage, low price
- Better tier: full features, reasonable limits (anchor price, most conversions)
- Best tier: everything, advanced features, 2-3x Better price

## Signs It's Time to Raise Prices
- Prospects don't flinch at price
- "It's so cheap!" feedback from customers
- Very high conversion rates (>40%)
- Very low churn (<3% monthly)
