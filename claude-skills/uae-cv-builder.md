# UAE CV Builder
### Premium UAE / GCC Executive ATS Resume Skill

Created by Shrinivas Ramaprasad.

---

## What this skill does

Analyses an uploaded CV or raw career history and rebuilds it into a premium,
ATS-safe executive resume optimised for UAE and GCC hiring standards.

---

## Target roles

- Program Manager / Senior Program Manager
- PMO Lead / Head of PMO
- IT Service Delivery Manager / Director
- Digital Transformation Lead
- IT Operations Manager
- Governance and Compliance Lead
- IT Director / Head of IT
- Enterprise Delivery and Consulting Leader

---

## Mandatory document structure (14 sections in order)

1. Full Name
2. Contact Information Line
3. Location + Portfolio / GitHub / Website
4. Executive Positioning Headline
5. Horizontal Divider Line
6. ATS Keyword Branding Strip
7. Executive Summary
8. Key Achievements
9. Professional Experience
10. Education
11. Skills
12. Innovation and Emerging Technology Projects
13. Awards and Recognition
14. Certifications

---

## Visual formatting rules

- ATS-safe only: no tables, no columns, no graphics, no icons, no text boxes, no sidebars.
- Primary colour: dark navy blue for name, section headers, and divider lines.
- Name: Largest font, bold, ALL CAPS, centre-aligned.
- Section headers: ALL CAPS, bold, dark navy blue, thin divider underneath.

---

## Bullet writing formula

```
[Strong Action Verb] + [Strategic Initiative] + [Metric] + [Business Impact] + [Enterprise Context]
```

Approved executive action verbs:
Orchestrated | Established | Directed | Led | Implemented | Streamlined | Embedded |
Delivered | Optimised | Spearheaded | Accelerated | Governed | Enhanced | Transformed
